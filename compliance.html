Regulatory, Risks & Compliance

We align our delivery and operations with leading regulatory frameworks, security guidelines, and risk management best practices—so your solutions stay secure, compliant, and fit for the UAE and global markets.

  • Alignment with UAE Cyber Security Council Guidelines

    Our solutions and practices align with the UAE Cyber Security Council’s guidelines and national cybersecurity strategy, helping you meet local regulatory expectations and protect critical assets.

  • Data Residency and Sovereignty Compliance

    We design and implement architectures that respect data residency and sovereignty requirements, including UAE and regional mandates, so your data stays where it is required and remains compliant.

  • Information Service Management Standards and Frameworks

    We apply established standards and frameworks—such as ITIL, ISO 20000, and related ISM practices—to deliver consistent, measurable service quality and continuous improvement.

  • Vendor Governance

    Structured vendor governance ensures third-party products and services are selected, monitored, and managed in line with your risk appetite and compliance obligations.

  • IT Risk Management Best Practices

    We embed IT risk management best practices across the lifecycle—from assessment and treatment to monitoring and reporting—so risks are identified, owned, and managed effectively.
